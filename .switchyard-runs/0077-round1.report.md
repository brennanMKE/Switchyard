Add SlidingTabs to the app target by tag
========================================

Added SlidingTabs (MIT, https://github.com/brennanMKE/SlidingTabs) to the Switchyard app
target of `Switchyard.xcodeproj` as a remote package pinned from 1.0.0, by editing
`Switchyard.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj` directly with the three insertions transcribed in
issue #0077. Nothing else in the project was touched; YardKit/Package.swift is unchanged.

**Invented ids** (both verified absent before use with `grep -c` → 0):

- Package reference: `A1B2C3D4E5F6789012ABCDEF` (XCRemoteSwiftPackageReference "SlidingTabs")
- Product dependency: `F9E8D7C6B5A4938271605F4E` (XCSwiftPackageProductDependency "SlidingTabs")

**Product name confirmed** by shallow-cloning tag 1.0.0 to `build/slidingtabs-check` and
reading its Package.swift: `.library(name: "SlidingTabs", targets: ["SlidingTabs"])` — so
`productName = SlidingTabs` matches exactly.

**The three insertions:** (1) a new `XCRemoteSwiftPackageReference` section beside the
existing local one, with `repositoryURL = "https://github.com/brennanMKE/SlidingTabs"` and
`kind = upToNextMajorVersion; minimumVersion = 1.0.0`; (2) a second entry in the project's
`packageReferences`; (3) the `XCSwiftPackageProductDependency` entry (with the `package =`
line, which local products omit) plus a fourth entry on the **Switchyard app target's**
`packageProductDependencies` (which listed YardGit, YardKit, YardCommands). Not added to
BrokerAgent or either test target — their `packageProductDependencies` are untouched.

Note: the issue's transcription said the app target's list "currently lists YardGit and
YardKit"; it also lists `CCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCCC /* YardCommands */` from an earlier round.
I appended after it.
