Widen the 60 s pending polls to the load bound

Round 2 of #0351: ReviewDecisionNoteTests failed 3-for-3 in a saturated run
(`pendings → nil` at its three 60 s `AppConnection.poll` calls). Same
diagnosis as round 1 — cooperative-pool resumption latency under full-suite
load reaches tens of seconds, so wall-clock deadlines of 60 s expire before
the delivery chain lands. No production code changed, no tests added.

## What was widened

ReviewDecisionNoteTests.swift (the named target):
- Three `AppConnection.poll` deadlines 60 s → 300 s (lines 48, 94, 152), each
  with a one-line comment citing #0351, matching round 1's serving-test
  precedent.
- The three non-timeout requests' `timeoutSeconds` 60 → 600 (lines 35, 85,
  143), round 1's exact pairing: the store's armed timeout is also
  pool-delayed and must never win the race against the test's resolve.
  `timedOutOutcomeRecordsNoNote` keeps its deliberate `timeoutSeconds: 1`.
- Step-2 check: the outcome await (`await outcomeData`) and the notes read
  (`ReviewNotes.list`) are unbounded — no other bounded wait in the file.

Sweep (`rg "seconds\(60\)|seconds\(30\)|seconds\(10\)" YardKit/Tests/`) —
round 1 missed four more files with the same shape, and the pre-change runs
caught two of them failing live:
