## Description

**Child of #0380.** **Question:** with the History `List` focused and a row selected, do ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓
reach menu-bar items carrying those key equivalents, or does the list consume them?

**Why it matters:** #0359 assigns Move Up to ⌥⌘↑ and Move Down to ⌥⌘↓.

### Build

Tab **0383 arrows** of #0380's spike app, plus **Spike ▸ Move Up** and **Spike ▸ Move Down**.

### Observe

1. Click row "c".
2. Press ⌥⌘↑ once, then ⌥⌘↓ once.

Record every new log line: `menu bar: ⌥⌘↑`, `menu bar: ⌥⌘↓`, and any `selection: …`.

### Pass or fail, and what changes

- **Pass** — each press logs its menu-bar line, and no `selection:` line appears. #0359 is unchanged.
- **Fail** — the selection moves (a `selection:` line appears, with or without the menu line), or
  nothing logs. #0359 changes Move Up and Move Down to ⌥⌘[ and ⌥⌘], the move-line-up/down pair many
  Mac editors use, and re-runs its shortcut-uniqueness test. Re-run this spike with those keys before
  accepting them.
