`Spike0417RepositoryTabsUITests.testRepositoriesOpenAsTabsOfOneWindow` failed:

**Hypothesis, not verified:** since #0418, the app registers the broker, and macOS posts an
"App Background Activity" banner at the top right of the guest screen. That is where the tab bar's
"+" sits. The banner is visible in the #0429 screenshot from the same run. A click that lands while
the banner is up would hit the banner, not the "+".
