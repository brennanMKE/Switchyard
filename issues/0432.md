## Description

In the full VM run on `main` at `2921aa81` (`build/ui-tests/20260927-011225-43851/spike-0417`),
`Spike0417RepositoryTabsUITests.testRepositoriesOpenAsTabsOfOneWindow` failed:

```
0417RepositoryTabsUITests.swift:83: XCTAssertEqual failed: ("3") is not equal to ("4") - the tab bar's + did not add a tab
0417RepositoryTabsUITests.swift:85: XCTAssertEqual failed: ("1") is not equal to ("2") - the + tab must be empty, not a second view of the launch window
```

Two immediate re-runs of spike 0417 alone passed, so the failure is intermittent.

**Hypothesis, confirmed by the planning pass (2026-09-27):** since #0418 the app registers the
broker, and macOS posts an "App Background Activity" banner at the top right of the guest screen,
over the tab bar's "+". A click that lands while the banner is up hits the banner, not the "+".

### Evidence (measured from the failing run's own result bundle)

`xcrun xcresulttool export attachments --path build/ui-tests/20260927-011225-43851/spike-0417/UITests-spike-0417.xcresult`:

- The `repository-tabs-plus-button` screenshot, taken after the click, shows **System Settings ▸
  General ▸ Login Items & Extensions** in front of the Switchyard window, scrolled to "App
  Background Activity" with Switchyard listed. That pane is exactly where clicking the BTM banner
  goes.
- The failure's screen recording (`Screen Recording 2026-09-27 at 01.29.45 AM.mp4`, 26.2 s),
  frames extracted with `AVAssetImageGenerator` at 1 s steps: at **9 s** the three tabs and the "+"
  are clear; at **10 s** the banner — *"App Background Activity — 'Switchyard' can run in the
  background. You can manage background activity in Login Items & Extensions."* — covers the top
  right (about x 664–1006, y 44–134 of the 1024×768 screen) with the pointer on it; at **11 s**
  System Settings is open. The "+" is `{{987, 81}, {30, 30}}` (the run's
  `repository-tabs-new-tab-tree` attachment), inside the banner.
- The log: `Click "new tab" Button` → `Synthesize event` took **5.5 s** (9.13 s → 14.64 s), the
  launch of System Settings, then 10 s of `tabs.count == 3`.
- The #0429 screenshots in that run are window-only (`app.windows.firstMatch.screenshot()`) and
  show no banner; the claim above that one did was wrong.

**The banner's timing is not predictable** — see the measurements below — so the fix cannot be
"wait N seconds"; it has to survive a click the banner takes.

**What the banner is, measured (planning probes in `../sy-plan-0432`, VM runs
`20260927-190716-19840`, `-191810-26028`, `-192104-28781`):**

- It is posted **during spike 0417 itself**, about 7–10 s after the spike's launch (every probe
  run saw it; the smoke test's 90 s poll on `NotificationCenter.windows` saw nothing, because —
  next point — it is not a window).
- In the accessibility tree it is **not** a `Window` of `com.apple.notificationcenterui`. It is a
  `Group` inside a full-screen `Dialog` titled `Notification Center`:
  `Group, {{664.0, 46.0}, {344.0, 89.0}}, identifier: '<uuid>', label: 'Login Items, App Background
  Activity, “Switchyard” can run in the background. You can manage background activity in Login
  Items & Extensions.'` — the same frame in all three runs, and it contains the "+"
  (`{{987, 81}, {30, 30}}`).
- **It persists**: still present 50 s after it appeared (probe run `-190716`, `probe-before-plus`
  screenshot shows it over the "+").
- **With the banner fully shown, a plain click on the "+" still added the tab** in both probe runs
  that clicked through it (`-190716`, `-191810`). Why is not established. The failure is the banner
  **arriving during the click** (the recording's 9 s → 10 s frames).
- **Clicking the banner reproduces the failure exactly** (`-192104`, a probe that clicks the
  banner's `Group` before the "+"): System Settings opens on the same Login Items pane as the
  failing run's screenshot, `banner.exists` becomes `false`, System Settings'
  `state == .runningForeground`, `tabs == 3`. Then `app.activate()` + a click on "+" added the
  fourth, empty tab and the whole test passed.

So a wait cannot fix it (the banner stays up and arrives at no fixed time), and switching to ⌘T
would drop the only coverage of AppKit's `newWindowForTab:` path, which is where #0417 found a real
bug (the "+" once opened a second view of the launch window). **The fix: if a click on "+" adds no
tab within 15 s, bring the app forward and click once more.** The banner goes away once clicked, so
the second click reaches the "+". This masks nothing: a "+" that adds no tab fails both attempts
(`tabs.count == 4` fails), and one that adds a tab late shows five tabs (`tabs.count == 4` fails).

