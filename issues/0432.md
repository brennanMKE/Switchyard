## Description

In the full VM run on `main` at `2921aa81` (`build/ui-tests/20260927-011225-43851/spike-0417`),
`Spike0417RepositoryTabsUITests.testRepositoriesOpenAsTabsOfOneWindow` failed:

```
0417RepositoryTabsUITests.swift:83: XCTAssertEqual failed: ("3") is not equal to ("4") - the tab bar's + did not add a tab
0417RepositoryTabsUITests.swift:85: XCTAssertEqual failed: ("1") is not equal to ("2") - the + tab must be empty, not a second view of the launch window
```

Two immediate re-runs of spike 0417 alone passed, so the failure is intermittent.

**Hypothesis, confirmed by the planning pass (2026-09-27):** since #0418 the app registers the
broker, and macOS posts an "App Background Activity" banner at the top right of the guest screen,
over the tab bar's "+". A click that lands while the banner is up hits the banner, not the "+".

### Evidence (measured from the failing run's own result bundle)

`xcrun xcresulttool export attachments --path build/ui-tests/20260927-011225-43851/spike-0417/UITests-spike-0417.xcresult`:

- The `repository-tabs-plus-button` screenshot, taken after the click, shows **System Settings ▸
  General ▸ Login Items & Extensions** in front of the Switchyard window, scrolled to "App
  Background Activity" with Switchyard listed. That pane is exactly where clicking the BTM banner
  goes.
- The failure's screen recording (`Screen Recording 2026-09-27 at 01.29.45 AM.mp4`, 26.2 s),
  frames extracted with `AVAssetImageGenerator` at 1 s steps: at **9 s** the three tabs and the "+"
  are clear; at **10 s** the banner — *"App Background Activity — 'Switchyard' can run in the
  background. You can manage background activity in Login Items & Extensions."* — covers the top
  right (about x 664–1006, y 44–134 of the 1024×768 screen) with the pointer on it; at **11 s**
  System Settings is open. The "+" is `{{987, 81}, {30, 30}}` (the run's
  `repository-tabs-new-tab-tree` attachment), inside the banner.
- The log: `Click "new tab" Button` → `Synthesize event` took **5.5 s** (9.13 s → 14.64 s), the
  launch of System Settings, then 10 s of `tabs.count == 3`.
- The #0429 screenshots in that run are window-only (`app.windows.firstMatch.screenshot()`) and
  show no banner; the claim above that one did was wrong.

**The banner's timing is not predictable.** Two planning probes posted nothing in the guest:
spike 0417 polled Notification Center for 43 s from launch (`20260927-190716-19840`), and the smoke
test polled it for 90 s (`20260927-191120-22245`) — no window other than the three desktop widgets
(`Featured`, weather, `Month`, all at x ≤ 368). So the fix cannot be "wait N seconds"; it has to
guard the click itself.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] The test is deterministic: it dismisses or waits out the banner, or presses ⌘T / uses the
      File ▸ New Tab menu item instead of clicking the "+" at a screen position the banner can cover.
- [ ] 10 consecutive `run-ui-tests-vm.sh 0417` runs pass.

Needs an Opus planning pass before dispatch.
