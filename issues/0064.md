## Description

After a `fixup`, `absorb`, or rebase, the question a reviewer actually has is "what changed in the
changes." `git range-diff` answers it, and the `post-rewrite` mapping (#0043) tells you exactly
which ranges to compare.

**This is the feature that makes the journal feel trustworthy rather than merely present.** A
reviewer who can see the delta accepts an agent's history rewrite; one who cannot undoes it
defensively.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] `yard rewrite-diff <journal-entry>` computes the ranges from the stored mapping.
- [ ] Many-to-one squash mappings produce a sensible comparison, not a broken range.
- [ ] Structured JSON output, not raw `range-diff` text.
- [ ] Works for rewrites performed by `git` directly, since #0043 captures those too.
